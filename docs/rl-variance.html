Phase 22 · RL variance study · concluded

RL can't be made low-variance here — but more harvest flips the mean.

Pre-registered question: can REINFORCE be made low-variance to match SFT? Answer, both levers tested: no. But the harvest lever (K=8) lifts RL's mean so far above SFT that, on expected value, K=8 RL now beats SFT on this substrate despite a wider σ — the variance gate was the wrong lens once the mean moved.

7B · HumanEval hard tail (idx 100–163) · passk=5 · pre-registered σ gate ≤ 0.056

01The setup

After the C4/C5 corrections, RL matched SFT's mean but carried ~3.3× the σ. The study pre-registered a locked criterion — pass@1 σ ≤ 1.5× SFT (≤ 0.056) AND no mean regression — then tested two levers on the exact same 64-problem ruler, with paired seeds and a base-vs-published sanity anchor to prevent another mis-measurement.

02Two levers, both fail the σ gate

The objective-side lever (GRPO's std-normalization) made σ worse (0.103 → 0.144): its ~2.3× larger advantage scale takes bigger steps and amplifies whatever the seed already controls. The harvest-side lever (K=8, doubling samples/prompt) moved σ the right way (0.103 → 0.076, ~CLT) but plateaus above 0.056 — a residual, non-harvest floor. Variance is harvest-driven, confirmed from both directions.

03The mean flip the story

K=8 didn't just tighten variance — it lifted the mean, because more harvest = more positive-advantage (RAFT-style) training samples per step = more learning. 6 seeds, same ruler:

Deployment math

K=8 RL is +0.174 over SFT; all 6 seeds sit above SFT's mean, and the lowest (0.447) exceeds SFT mean+2σ (0.438). K=8 RL mean−2σ = 0.386 > SFT mean 0.364 — even a pessimistic K=8 run beats a typical SFT run.

04Attribution — RL, not just harvest

Is the K=8 win from RL, or just from more harvest? The control — SFT with 2× harvest (samples=32) — is firmed to 6 seeds on the same ruler (config recovered from the surviving log and validated: the existing 4 seeds re-score to 0.385, matching the record).

More SFT harvest doesn't just miss RL — it hurts: 16 → 32 degrades the mean (0.364 → 0.329) and blows up σ (0.037 → 0.121). SFT-32 trails K=8 RL by +0.209 and is wider-variance too — K=8 RL dominates on both axes. The win is the RL regime (on-policy, reward-weighted), not the pile size.

05Deployment recommendation

Decided on mean − kσ, not the (now superseded) σ ≤ 0.056 gate:

  • Best expected pass@1 — K=8 posonly RL, 0.538 ± 0.076 (+0.174 over SFT), if you can afford ~2× the generation compute of K=4/SFT.
  • Tightest per-GPU-hour — SFT, 0.364 ± 0.037 (low variance, low compute).
  • pass@k inference scaling remains the training-free, orthogonal win, additive with either.
What's settled

Objective-side lever (GRPO/clip) dead × Wave 1. Harvest-side lever (K=8) doesn't make RL low-variance — it makes RL high-mean, and that's the better recipe on this substrate. Attribution resolved: RL, not harvest.
